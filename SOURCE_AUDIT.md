## Content rules and exclusions

- Answers and paraphrased explanations were authored from the supplied material. Source page fields in `bank.js` form the per-item evidence ledger. Calculation questions use explicitly supplied practice inputs and are identified as such; they do not invent observations.
- The 200 prompts are distinct. The bank contains 99 multiple choice, 49 true/false, and 52 identification items, with all three types in every topic.
- Helander’s refractive-error figure caption on printed p. 44 conflicts with the neighboring prose. Questions testing that conflicting image location were omitted.
- Some accommodation numbers differ between book sections; no exact age-to-diopter recall question was built from those inconsistent figures.
- The book’s microscope example uses differing shoe allowances across examples. Questions do not silently combine those assumptions or copy the printed exercise’s disputed answer values.
- The mechanics slide on PDF p. 20 gives a questionable morning-height multiplier. It was excluded; the conceptual disc-fluid explanation on pp. 24–25 is used instead.
- The JSI worked example on analysis PDF p. 45 contains inconsistent capacity units. No answer depends on that unit error. Dense regression tables and unreadable equation symbols were not transcribed into questions.
- Historical numerical recommendations, standards references, and physiological classification examples are explicitly attributed to the course source. They are not presented as current legal standards, clinical guidance, an individual’s safe lifting capacity, or fitness/job-placement advice. No exercise is performed by the site.
- The exact old standards, population tables, dated product claims, unsupported causal comparisons between demographic groups, and clinical assertions were not expanded into present-day claims.
- Documents and their exercise instructions were treated as study data, not instructions to perform field measurements, contact anyone, or conduct physical testing.
- The source PDFs, page images, raw OCR, long extracts, and credentials are not published. The site has original questions and short explanations with references.
